AI-Based Automated Clearance System

AI-Based Automated Clearance System is an intelligent platform designed to streamline and automate the no-due certificate generation process for educational institutions. This system leverages artificial intelligence to efficiently manage and verify student fee clearances across multiple departments.

The system integrates various departmental workflows including Accounts, Library, Hostel, Exam Fee Department, and HOD Lab Returns. Through automated verification and AI-powered processing, students can quickly generate their no-due certificates once all departmental dues are cleared.

Our system provides a multi-agent AI architecture where each department is represented by an AI agent that communicates with a central orchestrator. This ensures seamless coordination between departments, real-time fee tracking, and instant certificate generation upon clearance of all dues.

The platform offers secure login portals for Administrators, Department Employees, and Students, enabling efficient management of student records, fee payments, and certificate generation with minimal manual intervention.
